Help desk

Quick answers.

What does it detect?

IPv4, domains, URLs, MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256 hashes and email addresses. Defanged input like hxxp://example[.]com is cleaned up first.

Does it send my indicator anywhere?

No. Nothing leaves this page until you press Open, and then it goes straight to the tool you chose.

How do I add my own tool?

Settings, then add a name and a link containing {query}.

What about SIEM and ticket links?

They use placeholder hosts. Edit them in Settings to point at your own.
